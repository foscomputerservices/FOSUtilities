### Corpus boundary

T1 is declared eight times repo-wide: once in `docs/`, three times under `archive/` (49 Markdown files) and four times under `plans/` (80 files). `docs/` is the living truth; `archive/` and `plans/` hold prior versions, change logs and reviews, and are where the OQ keys resolve. ## What the census decides (session's reading, unratified)

**Build size: a day, not a week, for resolve, reverse and check over fosline's `docs/`.** Four declaration forms, one citation tokenizer with possessives and en-dash ranges, one index-line exclusion, one scope rule. Nothing needs a Markdown parser.

**Two grammars, not one.** Block keys (fosline) resolve to the declaring line and its block. Document keys (workflow) resolve to a file. Reverse and check are the same operation over both.

**Family is prefix plus document, not prefix.** R and Q force it. The declaration's file is part of the key's identity in the tool, even where the prefix is unique today.

**OQ4 holds, and its trigger has already fired outside the suite documents.** Text keys are enough for the nine core families: no number reused, tombstones kept. Supersession has bitten in the taxonomy (F7, F8, F18, F19 became A1–A4) and in the workflow corpus (spec-0001 reassigned). The MVP keeps text keys and reports tombstones, reassignments and cross-kind collisions as a finding class of `check`, so the evidence accrues where David can see it.

**Decisions the requirements page must carry.** Scope per corpus (primary and secondary). Tombstone semantics for resolve. Whether `check` expands ranges and reads `*Keys:*` lines as citations. Whether range claims are checked in the MVP. Whether a deleted work item resolves through git history. Whether a declaring sentence that changed since a citation was written is reported (needs the staleness fingerprint, parked by the work item).

```

```

Declaring-sentence drift per key across history: walk `git log --format=%h -- docs` oldest first, `git show <commit>:<file>`, keep the first 80 characters after each form-1 or form-2 key, and count changes between consecutive commits.
